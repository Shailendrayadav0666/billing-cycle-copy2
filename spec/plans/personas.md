# Personas — Mid-Cycle Subscription Upgrade

## Priya — Standard Subscriber
- **Role**: An active user on the $20/month Standard plan.
- **Goal**: Move to Premium mid-cycle without emailing support or waiting for renewal, and know exactly what she'll be charged today before committing.
- **Motivation**: She's hit her Standard chat-credit/chatbot limits and needs Premium headroom now.
- **Pain point today**: The Billing page shows a hardcoded "Standard" badge with no way to act on it.

## Devraj — Existing Premium Subscriber
- **Role**: Already on the $40/month Premium plan.
- **Goal**: Land on the Billing page and see his correct plan/quotas with no confusing "upgrade" affordance and no accidental double-charge.
